Source of the problem data

The 133 platform problems, their descriptions, itch scores and sub-scores (severity, market size, whitespace, frequency) were collected from Razorpay's public Fix My Itch page on the extraction date shown on the site. Ten further problems are editorial picks by this project and are labelled Editorial throughout. Razorpay has not reviewed, approved, sponsored or verified any blueprint, score or opinion published here.

How the ratings work

Platform problems carry Razorpay's published itch score out of 100. Editorial picks carry a weighted founder score: pain 30%, market 20%, timing 15%, feasibility 15%, moat 10%, revenue 10%. The source publishes some sub-scores out of 10 and others out of 100 depending on the sector; values above 10 have been normalised to a 0–10 scale for display. The full method is described on the site's Method section.

Opinions, not facts

Startup names, pitches, build plans, MVP roadmaps, pricing, go-to-market approaches, moats, risk lists, market estimates and verdicts are original opinion written to help a builder think clearly about a problem. They are not derived from, reviewed by, or endorsed by Razorpay, and they have not been tested in production. Market sizes, timings and cost estimates are rough orders of magnitude, not forecasts.
