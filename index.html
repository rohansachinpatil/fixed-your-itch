    How it was built

    Extraction. The Fix My Itch page renders its table inside Framer and only loads one category at a time. We drove a headless browser over DevTools, clicked every one of the 16 category chips, and read each row's React record directly — title, description, itch score and all four sub-scores.

    Coverage. 133 platform problems across 16 categories. Ten editorial picks from Automotive, Housing, Healthcare, Career, Hardware and Consumer Services bring the total to 143.

    Ratings. Platform problems carry their published itch score out of 100. Editorial picks carry a weighted founder score (pain 30 %, market 20 %, timing 15 %, feasibility 15 %, moat 10 %, revenue 10 %).

    What is ours. Problem text and scores: Razorpay. Startup names, pitches, build plans, pricing, go-to-market, moats, risks and market notes: independent analysis on top. Not investment advice.
