    How it was built

    Extraction. The official Fix My Itch platform by Razorpay renders its database inside Framer, loading one sector at a time. We drove a headless Chrome browser over DevTools protocol, scraped all 16 categories end to end, and captured every row's React state — title, description, itch score, and sub-metrics (severity, TAM, whitespace, and frequency).

    Coverage. 133 platform problems directly from Razorpay's Fix My Itch across B2B Services, FinTech, SaaS, Healthcare, EdTech, Logistics, Real Estate and more. Ten editorial picks bring the atlas to 143 complete blueprints.

    Ratings. Platform problems feature their authentic published itch score out of 100. Editorial picks use a weighted founder score (pain 30%, market 20%, timing 15%, feasibility 15%, moat 10%, revenue 10%).

    What is ours. The problem prompts originate from the public Fix My Itch wishlist. The startup solutions, MVP roadmaps, pricing models, moats, and risk audits are independent founder analysis built by Rohan Sachin Patil.

    Questions, answered

    What is Fix My Itch?

    Fix My Itch is a public platform by Razorpay where people and businesses in India describe real problems they want solved — from unverified plumbers to fake rental listings to medicines priced wildly differently across pharmacies. The platform scores every problem on severity, market size, whitespace and frequency.

    How many problems are covered, and where do they come from?

    133 problems were scraped directly from Razorpay's Fix My Itch across all 16 published categories, each with its description, itch score and four sub-scores. Ten editorial-only picks from sectors the platform does not publish separately bring the atlas to 143 problems across 21 sectors.

    Are the itch scores Razorpay's own?

    Yes. Platform problems carry Razorpay's published itch score out of 100, plus their own severity, TAM, whitespace and frequency ratings, normalised here to a 0–10 scale because the source mixes scales between sectors. The ten editorial picks carry a weighted founder score instead: pain 30%, market 20%, timing 15%, feasibility 15%, moat 10%, revenue 10%.

    What does each brief include?

    The verified problem statement, who feels it, a technical build blueprint, the first version to ship, the revenue model, recommended pricing, go-to-market strategy, defensibility and moat, the vulnerabilities that can kill it, and a founder verdict. Every brief can be copied as plain text in one click.
