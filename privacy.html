Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Applies to fixed-your-itch.vercel.app

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, runs no ads, and stores nothing about you on any server. The only thing it remembers is a single flag in your own browser telling it not to replay the welcome card.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No cookies. No cookie banner is needed because none are set.
  • No analytics or tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no Plausible, no Meta Pixel, no heat-map session recorder and no advertising identifier.
  • No accounts, no forms, no email capture. Nothing you type is sent to us.
  • No third-party ad or social embeds on any page, so no cross-site tracking pixels fire when you read a brief.

What is stored on your device

The welcome scratch card appears once. To remember that, the site writes a single key — fiy.intro.seen.v1 — with the value 1 into your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device, contains no personal information, and can be removed at any time by clearing site data in your browser. No other site data is stored.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard technical request information (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) to serve pages and protect against abuse. That processing is governed by Vercel's own privacy documentation, and those logs are not used by us for profiling, marketing or advertising.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font file, Google receives your IP address and user agent as part of that request. If you would rather avoid this, a content blocker or a browser setting that blocks fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com will stop the requests; the site then falls back to local system fonts and remains fully readable.

Third-party links

Briefs link out to Razorpay's Fix My Itch, to GitHub and to LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy practices and are not covered by this policy.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and no information is knowingly collected from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase on request. If you have a question about this policy, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
